Star icon credits

The star illustrations in DSP Seed Finder come from Some Suns, White/Brown Dwarf, Pulsar and Black Hole by Hansjörg Malthaner (Varkalandar), used under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).

The original transparent PNG is included unchanged. CSS selects regions, scales them to compact icons, and applies color adjustments to distinguish spectral classes. Giant icons use the flared-star artwork at a larger relative size. White dwarfs and neutron stars use smaller disks; a symbolic pulse-axis line is added to neutron stars. The black-hole artwork is rotated. These are illustrative UI symbols, not astronomical images or scale models.

This art is independent of Dyson Sphere Program and is not official game art. The artist does not endorse this project. The star PNG remains under its own license; the repository’s software license does not replace that license.
